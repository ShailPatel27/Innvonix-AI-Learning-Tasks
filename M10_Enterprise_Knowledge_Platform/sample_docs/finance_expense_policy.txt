Example Finance Expense Policy

This synthetic policy is sample data for the Enterprise Knowledge Platform. It is not a real company policy.

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business travel, approved client meetings, and work supplies. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days with an itemized receipt and a business purpose.

Team managers may approve individual expenses up to 500 currency units. Expenses above 500 require department head approval. Finance reviews complete submissions within ten business days.

Personal purchases, late fees, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable. Employees should contact Finance if a receipt is unavailable before submitting the claim.
